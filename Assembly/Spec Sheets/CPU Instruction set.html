Sheet 1: Operations
HEX NAME DESCRIPTION ALIAS(ES) 48 bit per inst.
0 ADC Adds <tar> to <sel>, outputs to <slc>; carry is outputted to CR
8 bit loaded at a time
1 ADD Adds <tar> to <sel>, outputs to <slc>

2 SUB Subtracts <sel> from <tar>; outputs to <slc>

3 SBB Subtracts <sel> to <tar>, outputs to <slc>; borrow is outputted to CR

4 MLC Multiplies <tar> with <sel>, outputs to <slc>; carry is outputted to CR

5 MUL Multiplies <tar> with <sel>, outputs to <slc>;

6 UNA Unallocated / No Operation NOP
7 PUS pushes <tar> to the stack

8 POP pops the stack to <slc>

9 BIS Branches if BR is 0

A BIU Branches if BR is 1

B BIC Branches is CR is 1

C BTF Branches is CR is 0

D BRA Branches to <tar>. Unconditionally.

E AND AND <tar> and <sel>, and output to <slc>

F NOT NAND <tar> and <sel> and output to <slc>

10 XOR XOR <tar> and <sel>, and output to <slc>

11 WIM Writes <sel> at address <tar>

12 RIM Reads from address <tar> writes it to <slc>

13 SAR Sets <slc> to <tar> SET, WRT, LDR
14 JSR Jumps to a subroutine (<tar>); Pushes to stack JMP
15 RET Returns from a subroutine; Pops the stack and jumps to read value

16 HLT Halt until the next interrupt STP
17 CMP SBB but only CR output DIF
18 SHL Shifts <tar> left; uses <slc>

19 ROL Rotates <tar> left; uses <slc>

1A SHR shifts <tar> right; uses <slc>

1B ROR Rotates <tar> right; uses <slc>
